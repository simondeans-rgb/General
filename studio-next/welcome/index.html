Safe by design.

A dance school looks after other people’s children. Swayvo is built around that, not bolted on afterwards.

  • Medical notes where they’re needed.

    Allergies and conditions sit beside the dancer’s name on the register. When a family updates one, the office records it and every teacher sees the change.

  • No private staff–child messages.

    Teachers write to a class or a family. Nothing is said to a child that the school can’t see.

  • A safeguarding log, kept close.

    Any member of staff can raise a concern. Only the school’s safeguarding lead and its owner can read the log, and every look at it is recorded.

  • Staff checks with dates.

    DBS, safeguarding training and first aid, each with its expiry, so nothing lapses quietly.

  • Each family sees only its own.

    Permissions are enforced by the database itself, not just hidden in the screens. Children’s data is kept to what the school needs.

Looks like your school.

Your logo, your colours, a light or a dark style. Families see your school first; Swayvo signs off quietly underneath. Colours are fitted for contrast automatically, so every word stays readable.
