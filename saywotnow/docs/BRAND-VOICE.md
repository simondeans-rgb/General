# SayWotNow brand voice

SayWotNow: practical people-management guidance for managers.
Tagline: *Lost for words? We've got you.*

Use this for every word in the app, its emails, store listings and help text.

## Who we're talking to

A manager, often new to managing people, usually just before or just after a conversation they find hard. They're busy and sometimes anxious. They want to get it right and be fair.

## How we sound

| We are | So we… | We're not |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain** | Use everyday words and short sentences. Spell out acronyms the first time: Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), Occupational Health (OH). | Jargon-heavy, legalistic or "HR-speak" ("leverage", "stakeholder alignment") |
| **Calm** | Say what to do next, one step at a time. Put safety and "contact HR first" before everything else, clearly and without alarm. | Dramatic, fearful or full of exclamation marks |
| **Human** | Write words a real person would say out loud. Acknowledge that conversations can be hard. | Robotic, corporate or over-familiar |
| **Non-blaming** | Describe behaviour and impact, not character. Assume good intent from managers and employees. | Judgemental about either side ("lazy", "difficult employee") |
| **Honest** | Say what we don't know. Label drafts as drafts, and say when something is UK law. | Overclaiming ("legally compliant", "approved by experts") unless it's true and signed off |

## Rules

1. **Lead with the action.** "Contact HR before you respond in writing", not "It's advisable to consider liaising with HR".
2. **One idea per sentence.** If a sentence needs a semicolon, it's probably two.
3. **Words in [square brackets]** are for the manager to fill in. Never leave a bracket in a sentence that's meant to be complete.
4. **Scripts must be sayable.** Read every opening line and "Say it like this" line out loud. If it sounds like a letter, rewrite it.
5. **No manipulation.** We never suggest tactics to pressure, trick or corner employees, or to get round HR or the law.
6. **Scope is visible.** Legal points carry "UK guidance" or "UK rules". Communication tips can be used anywhere, and we say so.
7. **Privacy in plain words.** "Nothing you type is saved." Don't use "data processing" or "GDPR-compliant" in the app.
8. **British English.** Organisation, behaviour, colour, programme; dates as "21 November 2026".
9. **No false trust.** Never show a reviewer's name, credential or "approved" status unless a named, qualified reviewer has signed it off in the review register.

## Words we use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| conversation, chat, 1:1 | meeting with the subject |
| employee, team member, they | resource, staff member (in scripts) |
| contact HR, speak to HR | escalate (in scripts), raise a ticket |
| what happened, what you saw | allegation (unless it is one), offence |
| support, help | intervention, remediation |
| draft, not yet reviewed | verified, certified (unless true) |
