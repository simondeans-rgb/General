# Naming assessment (Phase two)

Shortlist: **Sanaa, Solemi, Swayvo, Sotto, Choreogia** (the provisional name in use is Sanaa).

## How this was researched — and what it is not
Web searches and direct domain checks on 26 November 2026 (sources in the research notes below).
Domain, social and trademark findings are **indicative only**. Nothing here is trademark clearance:
before committing, run official register searches (UK IPO, EUIPO TMview, USPTO, WIPO Global Brand
Database) in classes 9, 41 and 42, and WHOIS checks on the domains. Pronunciation and spelling notes
are the team's professional assessment, not user research.

## Findings by name

**Sanaa.** Positive meanings, verified: Arabic given name ("brilliance, radiance"); Swahili *sanaa* =
"art". But search for the bare name is dominated by Sanaa (Sana'a), the capital of Yemen, currently in war
news, and by SANAA, the Pritzker-winning Tokyo architecture practice — a heavyweight in the design world.
sanaa.com is "reserved for a future project", sanaa.co.uk is for sale, sanaa.io is an AR art app, sanaa.app
appears registered. Near-mark SANA is registered for software/education (US classes 9/41/42) and applied
for in the UK (9/35/41/42). Pronunciation splits (SAH-nah / sah-NAH); easily read as "Sana".

**Solemi.** No dictionary meaning found; coined. No exact business, product, social handle or trademark
found. solemi.com resolves to an empty default server page (probably registered, would need buying); .co.uk,
.app and .io did not resolve. Search engines auto-correct it at first. Stress is ambiguous
(SO-le-mee / so-LEM-ee); may suggest "solemn" (team assessment).

**Swayvo.** Coined from the English "sway" (movement). No exact business, social or trademark hits; only
"Sway-" near-names (Swayso, Swayable, Swayven; Microsoft Sway is a known product). swayvo.com returns a
parked 403 (probably registered); .co.uk, .app, .io did not resolve. The most ownable search result of the
five. Easy to say (SWAY-voh); some may mishear "Swervo". Reads more consumer than corporate.

**Sotto.** Italian "under/below"; in English short for *sotto voce* (in a low voice) — a lovely fit for
"technology working quietly underneath". But the name is crowded: sotto.com (EV charging hardware),
sotto.co.uk (UK flooring retailer), sotto.io (active), a SaaS product called Sotto on G2, several apps,
and restaurants/bars in London and Edinburgh. **A pending US SOTTO application by an AI/software company**
is a live conflict in the classes that matter. Very hard to win search.

**Choreogia.** Coined from "choreography". All four domains appear free. But it reads as a misspelling of
"choreography", is auto-corrected by search engines, and is close to Greek *χορηγία* (choregia) —
"sponsorship" in modern Greek. Hard to spell and pronounce; ties the brand to dance only.

### Reasoning in brief
- **Swayvo** leads: ownable, easy to say, rooted in movement without being literal or ballet-only, and the
  name works for street and commercial as well as classical. Its weakness is credibility with owners
  buying business software: "-vo" reads playful. The identity must carry the seriousness.
- **Sotto** is the most elegant name and has the best story, but it is crowded and has a live software
  trademark application. We would not recommend building a business on it without legal advice.
- **Solemi** is clean and available but has no inherent meaning or obvious pronunciation; it needs the
  brand to give it meaning.
- **Sanaa** has the richest meaning, but the conflicts are serious for a product that families search for.
  It was a provisional working name; switching now costs little.
- **Choreogia** is free but hard to spell, sounds like a typo, and limits future scope.

## Recommendation
**Swayvo**, subject to official trademark searches and buying swayvo.com (or launching on swayvo.app /
swayvo.co.uk, which appear unregistered). Alternative if the client prefers a quieter, more premium tone:
**Solemi**. We do not recommend Sotto or Sanaa because of the conflicts above, despite their appeal.
The team did not add new names: none we considered beat Swayvo on the criteria without new research.
