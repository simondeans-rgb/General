Swayvo: three directions

You chose the name. Here are three complete, deliberately different ways Swayvo could look, sound and feel, each built against the same seven screens and the same school so you can compare like with like. Choose one, or a combination, and we start building on the preview site.

Concept B · Counts

Sporty, rhythmic, confident

Every dancer counts in eights. An eight-beat bar is the logo, the progress meter and the rhythm of the week. Bold ultramarine, wide headline type, big numbers where a decision hangs on them.

Concept E · Press

Communal, inky, full of life

Posters, battle flyers and recital programmes printed in two riso inks that overlap to make a third. Swayvo is the school's noticeboard: changes are set as big type ("MOVED", "CANCELLED"), not badges. Calm flat ink inside the product.

Recommendation

B · Counts remains the strongest all-rounder; E is the bold alternative

B · Counts is the most complete system for a product used daily by four kinds of people: clear, energetic, recognisable, and easy to build on. The refinements from the first round still apply (quieter office screens, warmer student and parent words, a stronger mark, a narrower type width in lists).

E · Press is the most original and the most "dance culture". Choose it if you want Swayvo to feel like a community brand first and software second. It needs a strong photography pipeline and careful work to keep owner screens calm.

D · Echo is the most premium. Choose it if you are selling first to established, high-end schools and adult studios. Its quiet palette is weaker for children and for scanning a busy timetable.

A coherent combination we would support: B's system with E's notices. Keep B's identity, colour and beat bar, and borrow E's idea that changes and announcements are set as bold type ("MOVED", "CANCELLED", "DUE 4 DEC") instead of badges. That gives parents the clearest notices of any version.

Once you choose, we write the brand guidelines, build the design system, rename the product (display name and assets only, not technical identifiers), and redesign every screen on the preview site first. The live demo stays as it is until you say merge.

Before launch, Swayvo still needs official trademark searches (UK IPO, EUIPO, USPTO, WIPO; classes 9, 41, 42) and a domain: swayvo.com looked parked; .app and .co.uk looked free.
