Recommendation

Concept B, Swayvo · Counts, refined with two borrowings

Swayvo has the best name to own and the most recognisable identity. The eight-count is something every dancer shares, from ballet to breaking, so it says "we understand dance" without a single dancer silhouette. It also gives the product a working graphic system: the same beat bar shows how full a class is, how far through the term you are and how a skill is developing.

We would refine it before building:

  1. Office screens borrow A's restraint. White surfaces, rules instead of boxes, one blue thing per screen, money set as a ledger. That answers the "is it serious enough?" question for owners.
  2. Student and parent screens borrow C's warmth. Kinder words, a goal in the student's own terms, one celebration moment, not the arc.
  3. Strengthen the mark. Widen the gap between the two groups of four and keep the peach downbeat, so it cannot be read as a signal-strength or volume icon; test it at 16px.
  4. Calmer type in the product. The expanded display width only for headlines and big numbers; a narrower width for lists so long class names don't wrap.

Your decision

What we need from you

  1. The name. Swayvo (recommended), or another from the shortlist. If Swayvo, we will also need you to arrange official trademark searches and a domain.
  2. The direction. B as refined above (recommended), A, C, or a combination you describe.

Once you approve, we will write the brand guidelines, build the design system, rename the product (only its display name and assets, not technical identifiers), and redesign every screen on the preview site first. The live demo stays as it is until you say merge.
